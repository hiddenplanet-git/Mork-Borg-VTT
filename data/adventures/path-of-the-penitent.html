The Path of thePenitent

A hex crawl in five stones

1 hex ≈ 6 miles North is up

What crimes have you done? Why do you seek penance?

Only you can know.

The Rite

  1. Set out from The Blackened Keep and walk the path to each of the Rune Stones of the Penitent.

  2. At each bloodworn stone, trace your fingers over the runes to unleash the test. It is usually a monster, a creature, or some other being called out of another realm.

  3. Fight, and move to the next stone. Or die at this one.

  4. Supplies may be procured in one place only: Broken Soldier Keep. Nowhere else on the path will feed you.

  5. When penance has been made at all five stones, go to The Chapel of Wretched Grace and have your atonement confirmed.
