TwoGuillians

A poppy field. A burning camp. One of them is lying.

Winds carry flower petals and embers. Screams sound from a field of poppies where thick black smoke billows overhead.

Two dying men, completely identical, deadlocked in combat, wrestling over a trident. Each claims to be Guillian. Each accuses the other of being an imposter.

What Walks Here

Star Mongrel

Monstrous quadrupeds with faces of finger-like organs that flower from a dripping central pit. Functionally blind, the face organs are highly sensitive to smell and touch, allowing it to navigate in complete darkness and track its quarry across great distances. Its massive burrowing claws and thick fur let it dig through the earth as quickly as a man can run across the surface.

If the darkness twinkles like a night sky against your torchlight, a pack already has your scent.

Scripture Worm

No natural thing, the scripture worm crawled its way into this world through the cracks of oft forgotten dreams. It nests within the throat of its host, spines weaving into flesh, sustaining itself and maturing until more worm than man remains.

It burns with dreamsmoke, manipulating its victims through visions and whispers, marking its scripture upon their flesh — runic rashes that portend the promises of the prophet.

The worm has been bestowed upon all of Sorcha's acolytes, granting them the ability to mold their squirming flesh into a myriad of appearances. They often assume the identity of others whose position may be of service to their prophet.

To make the imitation convincing, the worm may take the acolyte's memory of what they are.
